Questions

Hiring a personal chef in Atlanta

How much does a private chef cost in Atlanta and Gwinnett County?

My chef fee for a private dinner party is $300 for up to 4 guests and $500 for up to 8, a romantic dinner for two starts at $225, and a chef’s table tasting menu is $450. Groceries are billed at cost with receipts, so you pay what the ingredients actually cost. Weekly meal prep starts at $100 and cooking lessons at $175.

What does a personal chef do at an in-home dinner?

I plan the menu with you, shop for the ingredients, cook in your kitchen, plate and serve each course at the table, and clean up everything I use. You host; I handle the food.

Do you cook in Atlanta, or only Gwinnett County?

I’m based in Dacula and cook across Gwinnett County at no extra charge within about 30 miles, and I also cook in Atlanta, Decatur, Alpharetta and the rest of metro Atlanta. Those dinners carry a travel fee that I quote up front with your menu, so you see the full price before paying anything.

Can you plan a date night or anniversary dinner at home?

Yes. The Romantic Dinner for Two is a plated three or five course dinner for anniversaries, birthdays, proposals or a date night in, and I work around any surprise plans.

Do you offer weekly meal prep?

Yes. I cook a week of entrees and sides in your kitchen on a set day, portioned and labeled, or stock your freezer in one batch cooking session. Start with a free 30-minute meal prep consultation.

Can you cook for allergies and special diets?

Yes: vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, low-carb and allergy-friendly menus. Send every guest’s needs at least 7 days ahead. I’m ServSafe certified and keep allergen-free dishes separate.

Have a date in mind?

Tell me the date, guest count and occasion. I reply within a day.

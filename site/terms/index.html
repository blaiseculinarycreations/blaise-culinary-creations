Booking Terms — Blaise Culinary Creations

Your dining experience

Every booking is a custom menu built around your tastes, your dietary needs, and the occasion, cooked and plated fresh in your home. Your chef fee covers menu planning, shopping, preparation, service, and cleanup. You relax and enjoy the evening; everything else is handled.

How booking works

  • Deposit. A deposit reserves your date and goes toward your total. Once it's received, your date is held.
  • Your menu. We plan your menu together after booking, around what you love and any allergies or dietary needs.
  • Groceries. Ingredients are billed at cost. You're charged an estimate up front and only ever pay for what's actually spent, settled against receipts, so you never overpay for food.
  • Ingredient sourcing. All ingredients are selected and purchased by your chef to ensure quality, freshness, and food safety. Client-provided groceries or ingredients aren't used. The one exception is wine or other alcohol for a pairing, which you provide and purchase yourself.
  • Final payment. Your remaining balance and grocery estimate are due four days before your event, so your ingredients can be bought fresh with your menu in hand. Full payment must be received by your event date for the event to proceed.

Rescheduling

Life happens, and we want to be flexible.

  • You may move your event to a new date within 90 days of your current event date.
  • You're welcome to reschedule up to two times when a change is requested close to the event (within 14 days of the date). Changes requested with more notice than that don't count toward this limit.
  • Need a date further out than 90 days? Just ask. We'll do our best to accommodate a later date at our discretion.

Cancellation

  • 14 or more days before your event: full refund of your deposit, or reschedule.
  • 7 to 13 days before your event: 50% of your deposit refunded, or reschedule.
  • Within 7 days of your event: the deposit is non-refundable; you're welcome to reschedule instead.

If final payment isn't received

If your balance isn't paid by your event date, the event won't proceed and the deposit is retained. Any groceries already purchased for your menu will be provided to you.

Wine and alcohol

  • Wine Menu Curation is a fee for knowledge, not alcohol. It covers my time choosing wines to match your menu and writing pairing notes. It's a flat fee, the same whatever bottles you choose or what they cost.
  • You buy the wine. I don't sell, supply, buy, deliver or store alcohol, and it never appears on my invoices or grocery receipts. I'll send you a bottle list or a store cart you check out yourself from a licensed retailer, and the bottles are your property.
  • 21 and over only. Alcohol is served only to guests 21 or older. As the host, you confirm every guest served is of legal age. I may decline to pour for anyone who appears to be under 21 or intoxicated.
  • You're the host. You're responsible for your guests' alcohol consumption and for making sure everyone gets home safely.

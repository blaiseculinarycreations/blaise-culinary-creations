About the chef

Clauvys Blaise

I trained at the Auguste Escoffier School of Culinary Arts and cooked on the line, in catering kitchens and at university dining stations on Long Island before bringing my table to Georgia.

My dinners began with friends and grew by word of mouth. I write each menu like a story: a theme, a twist, and a reason every plate looks the way it does. The logo says it plainly. A dinner should make you wonder what's under the cloche.

Next I'm working toward a twelve-course tasting menu and pop-up dinners. Book now and you'll be at the table early.
